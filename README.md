# Raydius

Four-timbre virtual analog synth plugin with vocoder, arpeggiator and step sequencers. Free.

<img width="2142" height="1296" alt="Raydius 1 main" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6e3865ac-4fac-4359-8719-4012d7786fe9" />

Raydius is modeled on the Korg RADIAS of 2006: four timbres per program, each with two oscillators, two filters,
three envelopes, two LFOs, three mod sequences and two insert effects, plus a master effect, an arpeggiator, two step
sequencers and a 16-band vocoder. Every parameter sits on our own panel, over nine pages.

## Download

**[Raydius for Windows and macOS](https://files.re-style.nl/api/free.php?p=raydius)**: one zip with both
installers and the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md). VST3 on Windows (64-bit); VST3 and AU on macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon and Intel).

## The plugin

- **Every parameter on the panel.** Sound, modulation, effects, arpeggiator, step sequencers and vocoder, each on
  its own page; the four timbres one click apart.
- **Follows your DAW's tempo.** Switch on DAW under the Tempo knob: the arpeggiator, step and mod sequencers, synced
  LFOs and delays run at your DAW's tempo and stay on its beat, through tempo changes, loops and locates.
- **Audio input.** Send audio from your DAW into Raydius and play it through the vocoder, or through a timbre's
  filters and effects, as with the RADIAS's audio inputs. The envelope follower listens to it too.
- **The factory programs and your own.** The 256 factory programs, plus your own .syx and .rdl files: drop one on
  the program page or use its LOAD button. They stay in 256 user places.
- **Save your own sounds.** SAVE on the program page puts your sound in a user place and writes a .syx file, which you
  can share or send to a RADIAS.
- **Copy, swap and init timbres.** Copy one timbre over another, also from another program, swap two, or start
  one from scratch.
- **Your edits are kept.** A project brings back the program as you left it, step data included, with the window open
  or closed. Undo and redo bring back the exact sound.
- **Quiet when silent.** A Raydius that plays nothing uses almost no CPU.

## Setup

1. Run the installer for your computer.
2. Download these two free files from Korg's website:
   - "RADIAS System Update 2.0"
   - "RADIAS Artist Programs V2"
3. Unzip both into `Documents/Rave Generation/Raydius/Data`. Raydius finds what it needs inside them.
   (No such folder yet? Make it, or load Raydius once and it makes it.)
4. Load Raydius. The first start takes a little longer. If a file is missing, Raydius says so on its screen.

Own a RADIAS? The DWGS waves and drum kits need its wave memory. The zip has a small kit (Raydius PCM Dump Kit) that
copies it from your own unit.
